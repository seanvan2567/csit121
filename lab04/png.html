About This Image

This image is the logo of the Baltimore Ravens NFL team. It shows the head of a raven, outlined in purple with a gold "B" on the side of its head, representing Baltimore.

Why PNG Format?

The PNG format works perfect for this image because it provides a transparent background which allows for this logo to be added to many forms of media effortlessly, as well as having lossless compression that will keep the lines sharp across platforms and downloads.
