Reflection

This was a fun tierlist to make. The Office is one of my favorite shows, one I've watched many times over the years. I had never ranked the characters before so I had to put some thought into it. This ranking is based on a few factors, mainly how much I like the character, how complex/well written they are, and how much enjoyment and comedy they bring to the show.
